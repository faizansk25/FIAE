# Support FIAE Development

FIAE is free to use and source-available. If it provides value to you, please consider supporting its development.

## Why Donate?

Your donation helps:
- Maintain and improve FIAE
- Fund security audits
- Develop new features
- Keep FIAE free for everyone

## Donation Platforms

| Platform | Link | Type |
|----------|------|------|
| GitHub Sponsors | [Sponsor](https://github.com/sponsors/fiae) | Monthly |
| Ko-fi | [Donate](https://ko-fi.com/fiae) | One-time |

| Tier | Amount | Rewards |

## One-Time Donations

Prefer one-time support? Use [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/fiae) to buy the maintainer a coffee!

## Corporate Sponsorship

For companies using FIAE in production, corporate sponsorships include:
- Priority support
- Custom feature development
- Training sessions
- SLA guarantees

Contact us at sponsorship@fiae.dev for details.

## Transparency

All donations go directly to FIAE development. We publish quarterly transparency reports showing:
- Total donations received
- Funds allocated
- Development milestones achieved

## Thank You!

Every donation, no matter the size, helps keep FIAE sustainable. Thank you for your support! ❤️
